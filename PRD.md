# TraceX AI — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Product Overview

### Product Name
TraceX AI

### Tagline
Trace. Verify. Export.

### Product Type
Agentic AI + Supply-Chain Intelligence + ExportTech

### Target Users
Primary users are exporters, especially textile and apparel exporters, who manage fragmented product, supplier, traceability, compliance, and export documentation.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Exporters often manage critical information across disconnected documents such as:

- Supplier registration records
- Material declarations
- Product specification sheets
- Laboratory reports
- Quality inspection records
- Origin documentation
- Production and batch records
- Environmental records
- Export certificates

Because this evidence is fragmented, exporters may struggle to determine:

- Which claims are actually supported?
- Which documents are missing?
- How complete is the supply-chain traceability?
- Which compliance areas require attention?
- Which findings require human review?
- What should be fixed before export?

A conventional document chatbot may summarize documents, but it does not provide a structured evidence-to-readiness workflow with deterministic risk and verification.

---

## 3. Product Vision

TraceX AI aims to transform fragmented export evidence into a single, verified, explainable export intelligence layer.

### Vision

> Turn fragmented export evidence into a verified, risk-aware digital product passport and a clear export-readiness action plan.

---

## 4. Product Goal

TraceX AI should help an exporter answer five core questions:

1. What is verified?
2. What is missing?
3. What is traceable?
4. What requires human review?
5. What should be fixed next?

---

## 5. Proposed Solution

TraceX AI processes product and supplier evidence through a multi-agent workflow.

### Core Workflow
