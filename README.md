## 🎯 Problem

Exporters often manage product and supply-chain evidence across disconnected documents such as:

- Supplier records
- Material declarations
- Quality reports
- Origin documents
- Production and batch records
- Export certificates
- Environmental records

This fragmentation makes it difficult to answer:

- What evidence is actually verified?
- What information is missing?
- Is the product traceable across the supply chain?
- Which compliance areas need attention?
- What requires human review?
- What should the exporter fix next?

Traditional document workflows do not provide a unified, evidence-first intelligence layer.

---

## 💡 Solution

TraceX AI uses a **multi-agent AI workflow** to turn fragmented export evidence into a structured and explainable export intelligence result.

### Core workflow

```text
Product / Supplier Evidence
            ↓
      Evidence Agent
            ↓
   Traceability Agent
            ↓
     Compliance + RAG
            ↓
   Deterministic Risk
            ↓
    Verification Agent
            ↓
 Digital Product Passport
            ↓
 Export Readiness Action Plan
